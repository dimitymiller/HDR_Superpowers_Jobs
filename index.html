ABOUT THE BOARD

Research, industry, and everything in between.

Built as a starting point for researchers and students exploring careers in Australia, with a small number of international examples. Listings are selected manually, not collected from a live jobs API. We include positions that don't require a PhD, as well as roles where doctoral research experience is particularly relevant.
